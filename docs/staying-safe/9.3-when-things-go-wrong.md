# When Things Go Wrong

<Badge type="tip" text="Beginner" /> <Badge type="info" text="8 min read" />

::: info 🎯 What you will learn
- The step-by-step complaint ladder for investment-related grievances
- What SCORES and SMART ODR are, and when to use each
- What documents to keep ready when raising a complaint
- General timelines to expect during the resolution process
:::

## In plain words

Think of resolving a serious issue at school: first raising it with the class teacher,
then escalating to the principal if unresolved, and finally to a higher school board if
still unresolved. Investment complaints in India follow a similar structured ladder,
designed to give every level a fair chance to resolve the issue before escalating
further.

## How it works

### Step 1: Raise it with the entity directly

Your first step is always to raise the complaint directly with the broker, AMC,
distributor, or RTA involved, through their official grievance or customer support
channel.

1. Clearly describe the issue, including dates, amounts, and any reference numbers.
2. Request a written acknowledgment and a specific resolution timeline.
3. Keep records of all communication, including dates and names of representatives
   spoken to.

::: warning 📅 Check the latest
Each entity's specific grievance escalation timelines can vary and are governed by SEBI's
overall grievance redressal framework. Check the specific entity's official website for
its current grievance escalation process before assuming a fixed timeline.
:::

### Step 2: SCORES

**SCORES** (SEBI Complaints Redress System) - an online portal maintained by SEBI, where
investors can file complaints against SEBI-registered entities, such as brokers, AMCs,
and investment advisers, if the issue is not resolved satisfactorily at Step 1.

1. Register and file a complaint on the SCORES portal, providing full details and any
   supporting documents.
2. The concerned entity is required to respond within a timeline set by SEBI's framework.
3. Track the complaint status directly through the SCORES portal.

::: warning 📅 Check the latest
[VERIFY: check SEBI's official SCORES website for the current complaint filing process and
the current mandated response timelines for entities, since these details are set by SEBI
circulars and can be updated.]
:::

### Step 3: SMART ODR

**SMART ODR** (Securities Market Approach for Resolution Through ODR) - an online dispute
resolution mechanism for the securities market, providing conciliation and arbitration as
a further escalation route, if a complaint remains unresolved after the SCORES process.

::: warning 📅 Check the latest
[VERIFY: check SEBI's official circulars and the SMART ODR portal for the current
eligibility criteria, process steps, and timelines for this mechanism, since online
dispute resolution frameworks are periodically updated.]
:::

### The complaint ladder, summarised

| Step | Where to complain | When to use it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The broker, AMC, distributor or RTA directly | First step, for any grievance |
| 2 | SCORES (SEBI Complaints Redress System) | If Step 1 is not resolved satisfactorily within a reasonable time |
| 3 | SMART ODR (online dispute resolution) | If SCORES escalation does not resolve the issue |

### Documents to keep ready

| Document | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| Account opening and KYC documents (Unit 2.3) | Establishes your identity and account details |
| Contract notes (Unit 8.1) | Evidence of specific transactions in dispute |
| Statements of account and holding statements (Unit 9.2) | Evidence of holdings and discrepancies |
| Correspondence with the entity, including dates and reference numbers | Evidence that Step 1 was attempted first |
| Screenshots or records of the specific issue, such as a failed transaction or unauthorised access | Supporting evidence for the complaint |

::: tip 💡 Real-life example
Divya notices an unauthorised transaction in her demat account.

| Step | Action taken |
|---|---|
| 1 | Contacts her Depository Participant's grievance cell immediately, with all relevant
    account and transaction details |
| 2 | Does not receive a satisfactory resolution within a reasonable time, and files a
    complaint on SCORES, attaching her correspondence and statements as evidence |
| 3 | If still unresolved after SCORES, considers escalating further through SMART ODR |

This is illustrative, not a forecast. It shows the general escalation pattern, rather than
guaranteed outcomes or exact timelines, which depend on the specific case and current
SEBI procedures.
:::

::: warning ⚠️ Common mistakes
- Escalating directly to SCORES without first raising the issue with the entity involved.
- Not keeping written records of communication at each step, weakening the case if
  escalation becomes necessary.
- Assuming a complaint is resolved just because time has passed, without confirming a
  final resolution.
- Ignoring smaller discrepancies, such as those found during the independent holdings
  check in Unit 9.2, rather than raising them promptly.
:::

::: danger 🚨 Safety check
Report suspected fraud or unauthorised transactions immediately, both to the entity
involved and, where relevant, to your bank, since delays can reduce the chances of
recovering funds or reversing unauthorised transactions. Do not wait to gather every
document before making the initial report; report first, then follow up with detailed
documentation.
:::

::: details 🧠 Go deeper (university level)
SEBI's SCORES framework was established as a centralised, monitored, time-bound grievance
redressal mechanism specifically for the securities market, differing from general
consumer complaint forums by focusing on SEBI-registered entities. NISM investor education
material describes SMART ODR as a more recent addition, aligning securities market dispute
resolution with broader trends toward accessible, technology-enabled dispute resolution
mechanisms, offering conciliation and arbitration as alternatives to traditional civil
litigation.
:::

## Quick recap

- Always raise a complaint directly with the entity first, keeping written records.
- If unresolved, escalate to SCORES, SEBI's online complaint portal for registered
  entities.
- If still unresolved, SMART ODR offers a further online dispute resolution route.
- Keep account documents, contract notes, statements, and correspondence organised and
  ready to support any complaint.
